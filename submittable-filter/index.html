What it does

Detects spam

Auto-identifies publications by name and counts how many listings each one posts. The worst offenders float to the top.

One-click blocking

Block individual publications or nuke everything with 3+ or 5+ listings at once. Hover/tap any listing for a quick-block button.

Flags new listings

Green "NEW" badges on listings that appeared since your last visit, so you can quickly spot what's fresh.

Keyword filtering

Hide entire genres (screenwriting, journalism, etc.) and submission types (grants, contests) with toggles.

Saves automatically

Your block lists and settings persist between visits. No account needed, nothing to configure.

Shareable

Export your block list as JSON and share it with other writers. They can import it in one click.

Tips

The first time you use it, scroll through a few pages of Discover listings. The filter auto-collects publication names and counts as you scroll. Then open the filter panel (the circle button at the bottom right) and check the Frequency tab to see which publications are flooding the feed. Block the worst ones individually or use the bulk buttons. Your settings save automatically and will be there next time.

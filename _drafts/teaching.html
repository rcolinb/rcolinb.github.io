What students valued

Knowledge & passion

Students repeatedly described subject knowledge and enthusiasm, especially when expertise was translated into useful explanations.

What I carry forward

Rigor and safety are not opposites.

The pattern that matters most to me is the convergence: students describe knowledge alongside approachability, challenge alongside support, and clinical relevance alongside room to ask questions. That is the kind of learning environment I am trying to build.

“He never made us feel dumb or less than, and I appreciate that in a teacher.”

Teaching practice

From judgment to judgment-making

I treat clinical judgment as a developmental capacity, not a trait students either possess or lack. My courses emphasize application, comparison, and decision-making rather than fact accumulation.

Before nursing, I taught at Cornell in human development, cultural psychology, cognitive neuroscience, leadership, and community organization. Across fields, the question has remained similar: what helps people revise how they understand a difficult problem?

About these evaluations

Results aggregate end-of-course evaluations from four sections of Adult Health I, Adult Health II, and Mental Health Nursing taught from January through May 2026. Overall instructor satisfaction is based on 52 responses; the 12 detailed instructor items are based on 48 responses each.

The qualitative themes were coded from 43 instructor comments. Theme counts are nonexclusive, so one comment may contribute to more than one theme. Quotations are anonymous and have been lightly trimmed or edited for punctuation and grammar without changing their substantive meaning.
